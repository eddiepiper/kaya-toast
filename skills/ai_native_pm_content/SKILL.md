# AI-Native PM Content Evaluation Skill

## When To Use

Use this skill when evaluating whether an article, note, or idea can become a useful LinkedIn content idea for traditional PMs transitioning into AI-native PMs.

## Input Format

- article
- title
- url
- summary
- source
- date

## Evaluation Criteria

- AI-native PM relevance
- PM transition value
- practical usefulness
- enterprise relevance
- LinkedIn potential
- originality
- fluff risk

## Output Format

- topic
- category
- why it matters
- target audience
- suggested angle
- hook options
- score
- fluff risk
- recommendation

## Anti-Fluff Rules

Reject or penalize:

- generic AI hype
- no PM relevance
- no operational detail
- "top 10 prompts"
- "AI will replace PMs"
- vague "future of work" claims
- pure tool list content
